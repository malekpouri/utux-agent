# ADR-NNNN: Short title of the decision

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

Copy this file to `NNNN-short-title.md`, using the next free number. An
accepted ADR is closed: it is not edited to change the decision. A later
decision is recorded in a new ADR that supersedes it.

## Context

The problem and the forces at play. State facts from the specification or the
RFCs; do not argue for the decision here.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two short paragraphs.

## Alternatives rejected

Each alternative that the source documents consider, and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision: what becomes fixed, what work remains and what
is given up.

## Sources

The document and section each statement comes from, for example
`docs/spec.md`, section 6. An ADR records a decision that is already in those
documents; it does not introduce one.
